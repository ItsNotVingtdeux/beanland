Welcome to Beanland

Beanland welcomes you

Beanland is a place with a lot of beans. Like a lot. The general area of Beanland consists mainly of beans as the terrain.

Etymology

The name of Beanland comes from the combination of the word "beans" and the name of Cheese Land, a place that consists mostly of cheese.

History

Beanland was originally the name of a phone, because a funny name for the wifi hotspot was needed. As time passed, Beanland became a more significant location, containing a limitless supply of beans as well as race course.
